# Video Representative Registry — Phase 1

This phase adds a provider-independent foundation for profile-based company video calls.

## Included

- Public directory of approved company representatives.
- Client video-call request form.
- Public and private representative profiles.
- Separate avatar and voice provider configuration.
- Knowledge-source and permitted-action controls.
- Authorization records for likeness and voice use.
- Clear virtual-representative disclosure and human handoff.
- Administrative meeting assignment and room-link management.

## Main routes planned

- `/video-representatives`
- `/video-call/request`
- `/admin/video-representatives`
- `/admin/video-call-requests`

## Compliance requirements

Virtual or hybrid representatives must remain clearly identified to clients. External faces and voices must have documented authorization or licensing. Recording requires separate consent. A human handoff must remain available.

## Next phase

Connect approved profiles to a WebRTC room provider, avatar provider, voice provider, company knowledge sources, and audit logging.
